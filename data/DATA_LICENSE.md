# Dataset attribution and license

The feature tables in this directory are derived from:

**ESTOGU: A Multimodal Motor Condition Monitoring Dataset for Fault Diagnosis in Electrical Machines**

Creators listed by the official record include Ahmet Demir, Kerim Örüklü, Murat Başaran, Sener Agalar, Semih Ergin, and Ataberk Necati.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18222578
- Record: https://zenodo.org/records/18222578
- Upstream license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The processed tables retain source filenames and are redistributed for research reproducibility with attribution to the upstream dataset. Modifications consist of deterministic windowing, signal degradation simulation, and feature extraction as documented in `scripts/build_features.py`.
